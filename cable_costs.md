## Notes

- Every route goes below 3,000 m, so each needs the one-off deep-water qualification. Below 3,000 m the design is a study only, with no type test.
- Full-load loss is cable I²R at maximum conductor temperature plus 2 × 0.86% for the converters. It is conservative.
- Fault outage is the expected days per year at half power (one pole out).
- Costs exclude cost of capital. Fixed operating cost is about €250–260 m/yr and expected repairs about €17–19 m/yr.
- Routing: least-cost path on the GEBCO grid decimated to 0.1°. It ignores shipping lanes, protected areas and national waters. Landing points are rounded town locations.
- Contingency is applied to cable, laying and joints per depth class, not to landfalls, converters or qualification.
- The spread between combinations is about 4%, well inside the uncertainty of an unbuilt design.
